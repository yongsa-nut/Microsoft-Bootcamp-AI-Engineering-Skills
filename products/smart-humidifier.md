# MistFlow Ultra Smart Humidifier

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso MistFlow Ultra Smart Humidifier
Product SKU: HA-MFU-WHT
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso MistFlow Ultra is a 4L ultrasonic cool mist humidifier covering up to 430 sq ft, with a built-in humidity sensor, auto mode, and Wi-Fi control via the Contoso Home app. An essential oil tray adds optional aromatherapy, and 36-hour runtime on low means fewer refills.

## Key Features

- **4L Water Tank**: Up to 36 hours on low; top-fill design for easy refilling.
- **Built-In Humidity Sensor**: Measures ambient humidity and adjusts mist in Auto mode.
- **Auto Mode**: Set target humidity (40–70% RH) and the unit self-regulates.
- **Wi-Fi Control**: Schedule, adjust settings, and view humidity history via Contoso Home app.
- **Essential Oil Tray**: Side tray for essential oil diffusion without contaminating the water tank.
- **Ultra-Quiet Operation**: ~28 dB — quiet enough for sleeping environments.
- **Sleep Mode**: Dims LED display and reduces mist to lowest setting for overnight use.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- **Hard water is not compatible.** Using tap water with high mineral content will clog the ultrasonic transducer and mist outlet over time, permanently reducing mist output. Hard water also leaves white mineral dust on surrounding furniture and surfaces. Always use distilled or filtered water. This damage is not covered under warranty.
- Does not support Apple HomeKit — Contoso Home app, Alexa, and Google Home only.
- 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only; 5GHz is not supported.
- No warm mist mode — cool mist only. Not suitable as a steam vaporizer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use regular tap water in the MistFlow Ultra?
A: Depends on water hardness. Soft tap water (below ~60 mg/L TDS) may work short-term. For moderately to very hard water (above 120 mg/L — most US households), mineral deposits will clog the transducer within weeks, reducing mist output and leaving white dust on surfaces. Always use distilled or filtered water. This damage is not covered by warranty.

Q: Does the MistFlow Ultra support Apple HomeKit?
A: No. Contoso Home app, Amazon Alexa, and Google Home only.

Q: What is the difference between Auto mode and manual settings?
A: Auto mode: set a target humidity (40–70%) and the unit self-regulates using its sensor, cycling on and off as needed. Manual mode: set a fixed mist level (low, medium, high) and the unit runs continuously regardless of ambient humidity.
