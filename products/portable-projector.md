# BeamLite Mini Portable Projector

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso BeamLite Mini Portable Projector
Product SKU: EL-BLM-WHT (White)
Category: Electronics
Product Description:
The Contoso BeamLite Mini is a 1080p portable projector with built-in Android TV in a 1.8 lb form factor. It produces 500 ANSI lumens with auto keystone correction, a 2.5-hour battery, and supports HDMI, USB-A, Wi-Fi 5, and Bluetooth 5.0. Native Netflix is not available due to DRM licensing; screen mirroring or HDMI input is required.

## Key Features

- **1080p Native Resolution**: Full HD 1920x1080; accepts 4K input but downscales to 1080p.
- **500 ANSI Lumens**: Best in dark or semi-dark environments.
- **Android TV Built-In**: YouTube, Disney+, Prime Video, and other DRM-compliant apps natively.
- **Auto Keystone Correction**: Adjusts for angled surfaces without manual calibration.
- **2.5-Hour Battery**: Full movie without a power outlet.
- **Wi-Fi 5 + Bluetooth 5.0**: Dual-band streaming and Bluetooth audio out to external speakers.
- **Compact and Portable**: 1.8 lbs; HDMI and USB-A inputs.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Netflix is not available as a native app on the Android TV build due to HDCP/DRM licensing restrictions. To watch Netflix, use screen mirroring from a phone or connect a laptop/streaming stick via HDMI.
- No 4K output. The projector accepts 4K signals via HDMI but renders all content at 1080p natively.
- 500 ANSI lumens is insufficient for bright or sunlit rooms; a darkened environment is strongly recommended for best image quality.
- Battery life decreases to approximately 2 hours at full brightness. Running at 75% brightness extends runtime closer to 2.5 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why can't I install Netflix directly on the BeamLite Mini?
A: Netflix requires Widevine L1 DRM and HDCP 2.2, which the BeamLite Mini lacks due to its projector optics. To watch Netflix, use screen mirroring from a phone, or connect a laptop/streaming device (Fire Stick, Chromecast) via HDMI.

Q: Does it support 4K resolution?
A: No. Native resolution is 1080p. It accepts 4K HDMI input but downscales to 1080p for projection.

Q: How large an image can it project, and from what distance?
A: 60-inch image from ~6 feet, 80-inch from ~8 feet (1.2:1 throw ratio). Maximum recommended is 120 inches at ~12 feet in a fully dark room.
