# CleanBot Navigator Robot Vacuum

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso CleanBot Navigator Robot Vacuum
Product SKU: HA-CBN-BLK (Black), HA-CBN-WHT (White)
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso CleanBot Navigator is a LiDAR-guided robot vacuum with 3000Pa suction and an integrated mop attachment for hard floors and low-pile carpet. It maps your home automatically and maintains a persistent floor plan via the Contoso Home app. Compatible with Alexa and Google Home for voice control.

## Key Features

- **LiDAR Navigation**: Precise room mapping with obstacle detection for consistent, repeatable cleaning paths.
- **3000Pa Suction**: High-power motor handles pet hair, fine dust, and debris on carpet and hard floors.
- **Mop Attachment**: Included mop pad attachment damp-mops hard floors in the same pass.
- **150-Minute Runtime**: Single charge covers up to 1,500 sq ft before returning to dock automatically.
- **Auto-Empty Dock Compatible**: Pairs with the Contoso Auto-Empty Dock (sold separately, HA-CBN-AED) to reduce manual emptying.
- **Contoso Home App**: Set schedules, view cleaning maps, track history, and adjust suction level remotely.
- **Alexa & Google Home**: Full voice command support. Apple HomeKit is not supported.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Does not support room-specific or zone cleaning — the unit cleans the entire mapped floor plan only.
- Apple HomeKit is not supported; Alexa and Google Home only.
- May struggle with very dark flooring — LiDAR performance can degrade on black or highly reflective surfaces.
- Mop attachment is not suitable for deep-pile carpet; remove the mop pad before running on carpet.
- Auto-empty dock is sold separately and is not included in the standard package.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I schedule the CleanBot to clean only my kitchen?
A: Zone or room-specific cleaning is not currently supported. The CleanBot cleans the entire floor plan it has mapped. You can use physical boundary strips (not included) to block off areas you want excluded.

Q: Is the auto-empty dock included?
A: No. The auto-empty dock (HA-CBN-AED, $99.99) is sold separately. The standard package includes a basic charging dock only.

Q: Does the CleanBot work with Apple HomeKit?
A: No. The CleanBot Navigator supports Alexa and Google Home only. Apple HomeKit integration is not available at this time.
