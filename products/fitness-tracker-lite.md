## Known Issues & Limitations

- No blood oxygen (SpO2) monitoring — for SpO2 tracking, see the FitPulse Ultra

- Sleep tracking requires at least 4 hours of continuous wear to generate a score

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the FitPulse Lite track blood oxygen (SpO2)?
A: No. The FitPulse Lite does not include an SpO2 sensor. It tracks heart rate, sleep, and GPS-based activity metrics. If blood oxygen monitoring is important to you, consider the FitPulse Ultra, which includes SpO2 tracking.

Q: Can I swim with this tracker?
A: Yes. The FitPulse Lite is rated 5ATM (water resistant to 50 meters) and includes a dedicated swim mode that tracks laps, distance, and stroke type.

Q: What is the difference between the FitPulse Lite and FitPulse Ultra?
A: The FitPulse Lite ($79.99) is the entry-level model focused on core fitness tracking. The FitPulse Ultra ($89.99) adds SpO2 blood oxygen monitoring, extends battery life to 10 days (vs 8), and includes a gyroscope sensor. Both share the same 1.1" AMOLED display and 5ATM water resistance.
