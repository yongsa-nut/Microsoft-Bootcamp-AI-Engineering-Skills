The Contoso VisionCurve 34 is a 34-inch ultrawide curved monitor built for professionals who need expansive screen real estate for multitasking, creative work, and immersive gaming. ## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will the USB-C port charge my laptop while displaying video?
A: No. The USB-C port on the VisionCurve 34 is a display signal input only and does not support Power Delivery. Your laptop must be connected to its own charger separately. If a single-cable charging-and-display setup is a priority, look for monitors that explicitly advertise USB-C PD (Power Delivery) support.

Q: Can I run this at 144Hz using HDMI?
A: No. HDMI 2.0 supports a maximum of 3440x1440 at 100Hz. To achieve the full 144Hz refresh rate at UWQHD resolution, you must use the DisplayPort 1.4 connection. The USB-C input is also limited to 60Hz.

Q: Is a VESA mount compatible with this monitor?
A: Yes, with the optional VESA Mount Adapter (EL-VC34-VMA, $19.99). The monitor uses a proprietary stand attachment that requires the adapter plate to expose the standard 100x100mm VESA pattern for third-party arms and wall mounts.
