# AeroStyle Pro Hair Dryer

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso AeroStyle Pro Hair Dryer
Product SKU: PC-ASP-BLK (Matte Black), PC-ASP-PNK (Blush Pink)
Category: Personal Care
Product Description:
The Contoso AeroStyle Pro Hair Dryer combines 1875W professional-grade power with ionic technology to reduce frizz and add shine. Magnetic nozzle attachments and a lightweight foldable design make it equally suited for home use and travel. Three heat settings and a cool shot button give users precise control over their styling results.

## Key Features

- **1875W Motor**: Professional-level power for fast drying without excessive heat damage.
- **Ionic Technology**: Negative ions break down water molecules and reduce static and frizz.
- **3 Heat / 2 Speed Settings**: Customize airflow and temperature for all hair types and styles.
- **Cool Shot Button**: Locks in style with a burst of cool air for lasting hold.
- **Magnetic Nozzle Attachment**: Concentrator and diffuser nozzles snap on and off quickly and securely.
- **Foldable Handle**: Collapses for compact storage in luggage or travel bags.
- **8ft Power Cord**: Extra length for comfortable use without stretching to reach outlets.
- **Lightweight Build**: 1.2 lbs reduces hand and arm fatigue during extended styling sessions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the AeroStyle Pro Hair Dryer in Europe or Japan?
A: No. This model operates at 120V only and is not compatible with 220-240V European outlets or Japan's 100V standard. Using it with an adapter but without a voltage converter will damage the unit and void the warranty. A dual-voltage version is not currently available.

Q: What happens if I return it without a nozzle?
A: Returns must include both the concentrator and diffuser nozzles. If either is missing, a $12.99 deduction per missing item will be applied to your refund. Additionally, returns are not accepted if the magnetic heat protector nozzle seal has been broken.

Q: Is the magnetic nozzle attachment safe during use?
A: Yes. The magnetic attachment system is designed for a secure hold during operation. If a nozzle feels loose before or during use, remove it and reattach until you hear and feel the magnetic click engage fully.
