## Known Issues & Limitations

- The 300ml reservoir provides approximately 60 seconds of continuous use at medium pressure. Users with larger mouths or those using the high-pressure Pulse mode may need to refill mid-session to complete a full-mouth flossing routine.
- Replacement tips (PC-APW-TP6, $12.99/6-pack) should be replaced every 3-6 months. The standard jet tip should be replaced more frequently with heavy daily use.
- Reservoir is not dishwasher safe — rinse with clean water after each use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will the 300ml reservoir be enough for a full flossing session?
A: It depends on your flossing habits. At medium pressure (Normal mode), the reservoir provides about 60 seconds of continuous water flow. Most users can complete a full-mouth floss in that time, but users with more teeth, larger gaps, or those using Pulse mode (which uses water in bursts) may need to refill once. The refill process takes only a few seconds.

Q: Can I use mouthwash in the reservoir instead of water?
A: Contoso recommends plain water for daily use. Some alcohol-free mouthwash formulas can be used in small concentrations, but alcohol-based mouthwash can degrade the internal seals over time and is not covered under warranty damage. If you want to incorporate mouthwash, rinse the reservoir thoroughly with clean water after each use.

Q: Is this suitable for use with braces or dental implants?
A: Yes, for both. The included orthodontic tip is specifically designed for use around braces brackets and wires. The standard and Gentle mode settings are suitable for use around implant crowns. Always start with Gentle mode and consult your orthodontist or dentist if you have concerns about pressure settings.
