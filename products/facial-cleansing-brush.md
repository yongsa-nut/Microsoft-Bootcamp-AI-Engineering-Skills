## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this brush if I have sensitive or rosacea-prone skin?
A: Use with caution. The Low speed setting and the silicone zone are gentler, but if you have rosacea-prone skin, consult a dermatologist before adding a mechanical cleansing brush to your routine. Mechanical stimulation can trigger flare-ups in some individuals, even at the lowest setting.

Q: How often should I replace the brush head?
A: Every 2 to 3 months, depending on how frequently you use it. Daily use will wear the nylon bristles faster than every-other-day use. The PC-GSF-BH2 2-pack ($16.99) provides approximately one full replacement cycle for most users.

Q: Is it safe to use in the shower?
A: Yes. The GlowSpin is rated IPX6, meaning it is protected against strong water jets from any direction. It is safe for shower use, but it is not rated for full submersion. Avoid submerging it in water (e.g., filling a sink and placing it fully underwater).
