# TempPerfect Smart Electric Kettle

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso TempPerfect Smart Electric Kettle
Product SKU: HA-TPK-SST
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso TempPerfect Smart Electric Kettle boils 1.7 liters in under 4 minutes and holds precise temperatures for up to 30 minutes — ideal for green tea, pour-over coffee, French press, or any brew that demands a specific water temperature. Five preset temperatures cover the full spectrum from delicate white tea to a rolling boil, and the stainless steel interior ensures no plastic taste or odor.

## Key Features

- **1500W Rapid Boil**: Reaches a full boil in under 4 minutes for 1.7L.
- **5 Preset Temperatures**: 160°F (white/green tea), 175°F (oolong), 185°F (pour-over coffee), 200°F (French press), 212°F (full boil).
- **30-Minute Keep Warm**: Holds selected temperature for 30 minutes after reaching target, then shuts off automatically.
- **Stainless Steel Interior**: Food-grade 304 stainless steel — no plastic contact with water.
- **Auto-Shutoff**: Powers off immediately upon reaching target temperature.
- **Boil-Dry Protection**: Detects an empty base and cuts power to prevent element damage.
- **360° Swivel Base**: Detaches from base at any angle for left- or right-handed pouring.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Not recommended for use in Japan — the kettle is rated for 120V/60Hz (US/Canada) or 230V/50Hz (UK/EU). Japan's 100V grid causes the heating element to run at reduced wattage, resulting in significantly slower boil times and potential long-term element stress.
- Not yet available in South Korea pending KC mark certification.
- The keep warm function adds to energy consumption; unplug after use if the 30-minute window is not needed.
- The exterior plastic body can feel warm to the touch during a full boil cycle — hold by the handle only.
- Temperature display is in Fahrenheit only; no Celsius toggle on the unit (Celsius reference in this guide: 71°C / 79°C / 85°C / 93°C / 100°C).

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I set a custom temperature, or am I limited to the 5 presets?
A: The physical controls offer the 5 preset temperatures only. There is no manual temperature entry on the unit, and the kettle has no app connectivity. If you need an intermediate temperature (e.g., 190°F), select 185°F and allow a brief additional heating period, or select 200°F and let it cool slightly before pouring.

Q: How long does the keep warm function last?
A: The keep warm function holds the selected temperature for exactly 30 minutes, then the kettle shuts off automatically. The duration is not adjustable. If you need water to stay warm longer, you will need to manually restart the keep warm cycle.

Q: Is this kettle safe to use in Japan?
A: It is not recommended. Japan operates at 100V, and this kettle requires 120V (US/Canada variant) or 230V (UK/EU variant). Using the 120V model at 100V will result in slower boiling (approximately 30–40% longer) and may cause long-term damage to the heating element. Contoso does not currently offer a 100V variant.
