## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this toothbrush if I have dental implants or crowns?
A: Consult your dentist before use. While many patients with implants and crowns use sonic toothbrushes without issue, the high-frequency vibration may not be appropriate depending on the type of restoration and how recently it was placed. Your dentist can advise on safe settings and modes.

Q: How often do I need to replace the brush head?
A: Dental professionals recommend replacing brush heads every 3 months. The PC-BSS-BH4 4-pack ($24.99) covers approximately one year of use. The bristles will visibly fan out when it is time to replace.

Q: Is the Rose Gold variant still available for purchase?
A: The Rose Gold variant (PC-BSS-RSG) was discontinued in April 2025. Some third-party retailers may have remaining stock, but those units are not covered by the Contoso warranty. For warranty-backed purchases, choose White (PC-BSS-WHT) or Black (PC-BSS-BLK).
