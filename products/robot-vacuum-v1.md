# CleanBot Navigator Robot Vacuum

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso CleanBot Navigator Robot Vacuum
Product SKU: HA-CBN-BLK (Black), HA-CBN-WHT (White)
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso CleanBot Navigator uses LiDAR-based navigation to map and clean your home with precision, adapting to furniture layouts automatically. With 2000Pa suction power and a 150-minute runtime, it handles hardwood, tile, and low-pile carpet with ease. Control and schedule cleanings from anywhere via the Contoso Home app.

## Key Features

- **LiDAR Navigation**: Laser-based mapping creates accurate floor plans and avoids obstacles
- **2000Pa Suction**: Powerful enough for pet hair, dust, and fine debris on multiple surfaces
- **150-Minute Runtime**: Covers up to 1,500 sq ft on a single charge
- **Auto-Return Charging**: Returns to dock automatically when battery is low
- **Wi-Fi & App Control**: Schedule, start, and monitor cleaning via the Contoso Home app
- **Voice Assistant Compatible**: Works with Amazon Alexa and Google Home
- **HEPA Filtration**: Captures 99% of particles 0.3 microns and larger

- No zone cleaning or virtual no-go zones in the app (planned for future update)
- Not compatible with Apple HomeKit

- Dustbin requires manual emptying after each session

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the CleanBot Navigator have a mopping function?
A: No. This model is a vacuum-only unit. It does not include a mop attachment or wet cleaning capability.

Q: Can it clean multiple rooms automatically?
A: Yes. The LiDAR system maps your entire floor plan including multiple rooms and navigates between them in a single session.

Q: Is it compatible with Apple HomeKit?
A: No. Current app integration supports Amazon Alexa and Google Home only. HomeKit support is not available at this time.
