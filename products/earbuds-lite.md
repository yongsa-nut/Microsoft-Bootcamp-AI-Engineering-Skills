## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do these earbuds have active noise cancellation?
A: No. The SonicBuds Lite use passive noise isolation through snug-fit silicone tips. They reduce ambient noise physically but do not have an ANC system. For active noise cancellation, consider the SonicBuds Pro ANC ($129.99).

Q: Can I use one earbud at a time?
A: Yes. Either earbud can be used independently for calls or music while the other charges in the case.

Q: What is the difference between the SonicBuds Lite and SonicBuds Pro ANC?
A: The SonicBuds Lite ($99.99) offer passive noise isolation and 5 hours of battery per charge. The SonicBuds Pro ANC ($129.99) add hybrid active noise cancellation, transparency mode, and extend battery life to 7 hours per charge (28 hours total with case). Both share the same 10mm drivers and IPX4 water resistance.
