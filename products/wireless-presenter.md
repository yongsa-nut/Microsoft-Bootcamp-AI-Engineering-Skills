# SlideMaster Pro Wireless Presenter

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso SlideMaster Pro Wireless Presenter
Product SKU: OP-SMP-BLK
Category: Office & Productivity
Product Description:
The Contoso SlideMaster Pro is a compact wireless presentation remote designed for professionals who present frequently. It uses a 2.4GHz USB-A receiver — stored inside the device when not in use — for a reliable, plug-and-play connection up to 100 feet from the host computer. A Class 2 red laser pointer and slide navigation controls are included in a 0.7 oz body.

## Key Features

- **100-Foot Wireless Range**: 2.4GHz radio provides stable signal across large conference rooms and lecture halls.
- **Plug-and-Play USB Receiver**: No driver installation required; receiver stores inside the device when not in use.
- **Red Laser Pointer**: Class 2 red laser for highlighting on-screen content during presentations.
- **Full Slide Controls**: Forward, back, and blank-screen buttons for smooth presentation flow.
- **Volume Up/Down**: Adjust system volume directly from the presenter without returning to the computer.
- **Integrated Receiver Storage**: USB-A nano receiver slots into the device to prevent loss during travel.
- **Long Battery Life**: Runs on 2x AAA batteries (included); rated for approximately 12 months of regular use.
- **Lightweight Build**: 0.7 oz makes it comfortable to hold during extended presentations.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Uses USB-A receiver only. There is no Bluetooth option. The SlideMaster Pro is NOT compatible with iPads, Android tablets, or any device that lacks a USB-A port without an adapter.
- USB-C adapters may allow physical connection, but HID compatibility is not guaranteed on all devices — test before presenting.
- No built-in timer, countdown display, or vibration alerts for time management.
- Laser pointer is not visible on projector screens in very bright ambient lighting; not recommended as a primary laser in outdoor settings.
- Volume control buttons adjust the host computer's system volume and may not work in all presentation software environments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will this work with an iPad or Android tablet for presentations?
A: No, not directly. The SlideMaster Pro uses a USB-A nano receiver and does not support Bluetooth. iPads and most tablets do not have USB-A ports and do not support the 2.4GHz HID protocol used by the receiver. Users who present from tablets should look for a Bluetooth-based presenter instead. A USB-C adapter may allow the receiver to connect physically to some devices, but compatibility is not guaranteed and Contoso does not officially support this configuration.

Q: Do I need to install any software or drivers?
A: No. The SlideMaster Pro is plug-and-play on Windows, macOS, and Linux. Insert the USB-A receiver into any available port and the device registers as a standard HID keyboard/mouse input. It works with PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, LibreOffice Impress, and most other presentation software.

Q: How far can I be from the receiver and still have a reliable connection?
A: The rated range is 100 feet in open, line-of-sight conditions. In practice, walls, interference from other 2.4GHz devices (Wi-Fi routers, other wireless peripherals), and physical obstructions may reduce effective range. In typical conference room environments with standard construction, 50–75 feet of reliable range is a realistic expectation.
