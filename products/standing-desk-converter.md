# LiftPro X Standing Desk Converter

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso LiftPro X Standing Desk Converter
Product SKU: OP-LPX-BLK (Black), OP-LPX-WHT (White)
Category: Office & Productivity
Product Description:
The Contoso LiftPro X is a gas-spring standing desk converter that sits on top of any existing desk, raising your monitor and keyboard to standing height in seconds. It features a split-level design with a dedicated keyboard tray and a 36"x24" main work surface, arriving mostly pre-assembled for immediate use. No desk replacement required.

- **36"x24" Work Surface**: Spacious main platform accommodates a monitor, laptop, and accessories.
- **Split-Level Keyboard Tray**: Lower tray positions keyboard and mouse at a comfortable ergonomic angle when standing.
- **35 lb Weight Capacity**: Supports monitors, laptops, and accessories up to 35 lbs total on the main platform.
- **Mostly Pre-Assembled**: Arrives ~90% assembled; minor final steps take under 15 minutes.
- **Stable Base Design**: Wide footprint with non-slip pads fits desks 24 inches or deeper.
- **Two Color Options**: Available in Black and White to match office aesthetics.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- The 35 lb platform weight limit is a firm constraint. Common dual-monitor setups with two 27" monitors can approach or exceed this limit — calculate your total monitor and accessory weight before purchasing.
- The converter raises the work surface but does not address chair height or under-desk clearance; users may also need to adjust their chair.
- Keyboard tray width (33") may not accommodate wide gaming-style deskmats or oversized keyboards with a separate numpad and wrist rest.
- Minimum 24" desk depth requirement means the converter is not compatible with very shallow desks.
- Not suitable for glass-top desks due to suction and weight distribution concerns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use two monitors on the LiftPro X?
A: It depends on your monitors' total weight. The platform supports up to 35 lbs. A single 27" monitor typically weighs 10–14 lbs, so two monitors may total 20–28 lbs — within the limit if you are not also placing a laptop or heavy accessories on the platform. Weigh your equipment before purchasing. If total weight approaches or exceeds 35 lbs, consider a dedicated motorized standing desk instead.

Q: Will this fit on my desk?
A: The LiftPro X requires a desk at least 24 inches deep and ideally 40 inches or wider to avoid overhang. The converter itself is 36 inches wide. Glass-top desks are not recommended due to weight distribution and suction-pad incompatibility.

Q: How much assembly is required out of the box?
A: The unit arrives approximately 90% pre-assembled. The main remaining step is attaching the keyboard tray to the lower platform, which takes about 10–15 minutes using the included Allen wrench. No power tools are needed.
