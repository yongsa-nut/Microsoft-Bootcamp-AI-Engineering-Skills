# TypeForce TKL Mechanical Keyboard

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso TypeForce TKL Mechanical Keyboard
Product SKU: EL-TFT-BLK (Black), EL-TFT-WHT (White)
Category: Electronics
Product Description:
The Contoso TypeForce TKL is a tenkeyless wired mechanical keyboard built for typists and gamers who want a compact, high-quality typing experience. Featuring soldered Gateron switches, per-key RGB backlighting, and durable PBT keycaps, it delivers consistent tactile feedback and long-term reliability. A detachable USB-C cable makes transport and desk cable management straightforward.

## Key Features

- **Gateron Switches**: Available in Red (linear), Brown (tactile), or Blue (clicky) — soldered for stability
- **Per-Key RGB Lighting**: Full RGB with 15+ preset lighting effects via software
- **PBT Keycaps**: Double-shot PBT for shine resistance and longevity
- **TKL 87-Key Layout**: Compact tenkeyless saves desk space without sacrificing core keys
- **Detachable USB-C Cable**: Braided 1.8m cable; easy to replace or route
- **N-Key Rollover**: Full NKRO for accurate multi-key input during fast typing or gaming
- **Onboard Memory**: Save up to 3 lighting and macro profiles directly to the keyboard
- **Aluminum Top Plate**: Sturdy construction reduces flex and dampens sound

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Wired only — no Bluetooth or wireless connectivity
- Switches are soldered; cannot be swapped without desoldering equipment
- RGB software (Contoso Key Studio) is Windows-only; Mac users limited to onboard lighting presets
- Blue switch variant may be too loud for shared office environments
- No dedicated media keys; function layer required for media and volume controls

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this keyboard support Bluetooth or wireless connectivity?
A: No. The TypeForce TKL is a wired-only keyboard using a detachable USB-C connection. There is no Bluetooth or wireless mode.

Q: Can I swap out the switches myself?
A: Not without desoldering. Switches are soldered to the PCB. If you want to try different switches, the current version requires a soldering iron to desolder and replace them.

Q: Is the keyboard compatible with Mac?
A: Yes for basic typing. However, the Contoso Key Studio software for RGB customization is Windows-only. Mac users can cycle through onboard preset lighting effects using the Fn key combination.
