## Known Issues & Limitations

- **Hot liquid warning**: Do NOT blend liquids above 160°F (70°C). Steam pressure from hot liquids can force the lid off and cause burns. Always allow hot soups or liquids to cool below 160°F before blending, and remove the center vent cap to release any residual steam.
- The jar is NOT dishwasher safe — heat and detergents degrade the Tritan material and can loosen blade gaskets over time. Hand wash only.
- Not available in EU countries or Japan due to voltage and certification requirements.
- The blender is loud (~88 dB at max speed) — not suitable for early-morning use in shared living spaces.
- The base is not waterproof; do not submerge or rinse the motor housing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I blend hot soup directly in the VortexBlend Pro?
A: Not if the liquid is above 160°F (70°C). Steam from hot liquids builds pressure inside the sealed jar and can eject the lid forcefully. Let your soup cool to below 160°F first, fill the jar no more than halfway, and remove the center vent cap before blending. Start on the lowest speed setting.

Q: Is the jar dishwasher safe?
A: No. The Tritan jar must be hand washed only. Dishwasher heat and harsh detergents can cloud the plastic, degrade the blade seal gasket, and void the jar warranty. The self-cleaning mode (warm water + dish soap + run cycle) handles most residue effectively.

Q: What is the difference between the Black and Red SKUs?
A: The only difference is color. HA-VBP-BLK is Matte Black and HA-VBP-RED is Matte Red. All specifications, accessories, and pricing are identical.
