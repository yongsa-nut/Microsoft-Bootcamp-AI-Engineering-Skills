# WatchGuard Pro Smart Video Doorbell

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso WatchGuard Pro Smart Video Doorbell
Product SKU: EL-WGP-WHT (White)
Category: Electronics
Product Description:
The Contoso WatchGuard Pro is a wired smart video doorbell offering 2K resolution, a 160° wide-angle lens, and two-way audio for real-time visitor monitoring. It integrates with the Contoso Home app for motion zone configuration and instant push notifications. A cloud subscription is required to access recorded video history; without one, only live viewing is available.

## Key Features

- **2K Video Resolution**: Clear, detailed footage day and night at 2048 x 1536 resolution.
- **160° Wide-Angle Lens**: Captures the full doorstep area including packages left to the side.
- **Night Vision (25ft)**: Infrared night vision provides clear imaging up to 25 feet in darkness.
- **Two-Way Audio**: Built-in speaker and microphone enable real-time conversation with visitors.
- **Customizable Motion Zones**: Define specific detection areas to reduce false alerts.
- **Instant Push Notifications**: Receive alerts on iOS and Android within seconds of motion detection.
- **Wired Installation**: Uses existing 16–24V AC doorbell wiring for continuous power — no battery to replace.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- No continuous recording — video is captured in 30-second event-triggered clips only.
- No local storage option; all footage is stored in Contoso Cloud. A subscription is required to replay any recorded clips.
- Without an active WatchGuard Cloud subscription, you can only access the live camera view — no footage is saved or retrievable.
- Wi-Fi is 2.4GHz only; 5GHz networks are not supported, which may affect performance in congested environments.
- Requires existing doorbell wiring (16–24V AC); not compatible with battery-only or intercom-only systems.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens if I don't subscribe to WatchGuard Cloud?
A: Without an active subscription, the doorbell still sends real-time motion alerts and allows live viewing through the Contoso Home app. However, no video is recorded or saved — you cannot review past events or footage. All recording and playback features require the WatchGuard Cloud subscription ($3.99/month).

Q: Does it work with my existing doorbell chime?
A: Yes, the WatchGuard Pro is compatible with most standard mechanical and digital chimes that run on 16–24V AC. It does not include a standalone chime unit. If your home lacks doorbell wiring entirely, this product is not compatible.

Q: Can I set it to only detect people and ignore cars or animals?
A: Yes. The Contoso Home app allows you to draw custom motion zones on the camera frame, limiting detection to specific areas such as the doorstep. The app also offers motion sensitivity adjustment, though object-type filtering (person vs. vehicle) is not currently supported.
