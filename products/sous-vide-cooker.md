## Known Issues & Limitations

- The IPX7 waterproof rating applies only up to the clamp line. Do not submerge the upper body or control housing — this is not covered by warranty.
- Not available in Japan (pending PSE certification), Australia (voltage compliance pending), or South Korea.
- The clamp fits pot rims up to 1 inch thick; wider rim stock pots or non-standard containers may not be compatible.
- Wi-Fi scheduling and push notifications require the Contoso Home app; the manual dial functions independently for temperature and timer but cannot send remote alerts.
- Long cooks (12+ hours) in shallow pots risk water evaporation below the minimum 2.5-inch depth; use a lid or cover the water bath with plastic wrap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need vacuum seal bags, or can I use regular zip-lock bags?
A: Vacuum seal bags (HA-PCI-BG50, $14.99/50-pack) are recommended. Standard zip-lock freezer bags also work using the water displacement method (submerge slowly to push out air before sealing). Do not use thin storage bags — only freezer-grade; thinner plastic may fail at higher temperatures.

Q: Can I use the PrecisionChef without Wi-Fi or the app?
A: Yes. The onboard dial and display provide full standalone temperature and timer control. The app adds remote monitoring, scheduling, notifications, and the recipe library, but is not required for cooking.

Q: What is the maximum pot or container size I can use?
A: Up to 5 gallons (19L). The clamp fits rims up to 1 inch thick. Maintain at least 2.5 inches water depth and do not submerge above the clamp line.
