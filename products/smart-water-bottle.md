The Contoso HydroTrack Smart Water Bottle tracks your daily hydration automatically using a weight sensor in its base, syncing intake data to the Contoso FitLife app. An LED glow ring provides visual reminders to drink throughout the day. ## Known Issues & Limitations

- Not dishwasher safe. The electronics in the base module are not waterproof to dishwasher conditions. Hand wash the bottle body only; wipe the base with a damp cloth.
- Do not fill with liquids above 140°F (60°C). The insulation retains heat effectively, but sustained high temperatures can damage the sensor electronics in the base.
- The smart base must be attached for tracking to work — the bottle does not track intake if the base is removed.
- Tracking accuracy depends on picking up and setting down the bottle; it does not detect sips if the bottle is tilted without being lifted.
- Bluetooth range is approximately 30 ft — phone must be reasonably close for real-time sync.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I put the HydroTrack bottle in the dishwasher?
A: No. The bottle is not dishwasher safe. The smart base module contains electronics that will be damaged by dishwasher heat, water pressure, and detergent. Wash the bottle body by hand with warm soapy water. Clean the base by wiping with a damp cloth only. Do not submerge the base in water.

Q: Can I use this bottle for hot coffee or tea?
A: The bottle can hold hot beverages, and insulation will keep them warm for up to 12 hours. However, do not fill with liquids above 140°F (60°C). Extremely hot beverages (e.g., freshly brewed coffee straight from a machine, which can exceed 190°F) should be cooled slightly before pouring in, as sustained extreme heat may damage the sensor electronics in the base module over time.

Q: What happens to my hydration data if my phone is not nearby?
A: The bottle stores recent session data locally in the base module. When your phone comes back within Bluetooth range (approximately 30 ft), the FitLife app will sync the stored data automatically in the background. You do not need your phone present during the day for tracking — data will catch up when you reconnect.
