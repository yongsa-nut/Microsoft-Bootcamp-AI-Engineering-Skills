## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso FitPulse Ultra Fitness Tracker
Product SKU: FT-FPU-BLK (Black), FT-FPU-NVY (Navy), FT-FPU-SGR (Sage Green - Discontinued)

Product Description:
Track every rep, step, and heartbeat with the Contoso FitPulse Ultra. Featuring continuous heart rate monitoring, built-in GPS, and blood oxygen (SpO2) tracking, this slim fitness tracker keeps you informed without the bulk of a smartwatch. The always-on AMOLED display is bright enough for outdoor runs, and with 10-day battery life in standard mode, you'll spend less time charging. Water-resistant to 50 meters for the pool, trail, or gym.

## Key Features

- Continuous Heart Rate Monitoring: 24/7 optical sensor with resting HR trends
- Built-in GPS: Track outdoor activities without your phone
- Blood Oxygen (SpO2): On-demand and overnight readings (wellness only, not medical-grade)
- Sleep Tracking: Automatic sleep stage detection with sleep score
- 20+ Exercise Modes including swimming, yoga, HIIT, strength training
- Always-On 1.1" AMOLED Display, 450 nits peak brightness
- Smart Notifications: Call, text, app alerts (display only — cannot reply from device)
- Water Resistant: 5 ATM (50m) — not for diving or hot water exposure

## Known Issues & Limitations

- SpO2 is for wellness purposes only — not medical-grade
- GPS lock may take 30-90 seconds in areas with tall buildings or heavy tree cover
- Heart rate accuracy decreases during heavy wrist movements (weightlifting, rowing); wear higher on forearm for better accuracy
- Always-on display reduces battery life by approximately 50%
- Band may cause irritation for users with silicone sensitivity; hypoallergenic bands not available

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I swim with it?
A: Yes, rated 5 ATM for pool and open water swimming. Not designed for diving, water skiing, or high-velocity water. Do not wear in hot tubs or saunas.

Q: Does it work without a phone?
A: GPS, heart rate, steps, and exercise tracking work standalone. Initial setup requires the app, and notifications, weather, and music control need a Bluetooth connection.

Q: Is the Sage Green color still available?
A: Discontinued March 2025. No longer available through Contoso. Third-party units purchased after discontinuation are not covered by warranty.
