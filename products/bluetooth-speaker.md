## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the Sunset Orange (EL-SWP-ORG) with the Contoso warranty if I buy it from a third-party retailer?
A: No. The Sunset Orange variant was discontinued in January 2025. Any units sold through third-party sellers after that date are residual stock not covered by Contoso warranty. Contoso recommends purchasing only the Black or Ocean Blue variants for full warranty coverage.

Q: How does TWS stereo pairing work?
A: Power on both SoundWave units and connect one to your device via Bluetooth as the primary speaker. Then hold the Bluetooth button on the second unit for 3 seconds while both are powered on — the speakers will auto-pair and split into left and right channels. Both units must run the same firmware version for pairing to succeed.

Q: Is the USB-C port waterproof?
A: The speaker is IPX7 rated only when the USB-C port cover is fully closed and sealed. The port itself is not waterproof when open. Always ensure the port cover clicks into place before exposing the speaker to water.
