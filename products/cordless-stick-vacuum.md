# SwiftClean V10 Cordless Stick Vacuum

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso SwiftClean V10 Cordless Stick Vacuum
Product SKU: HA-SCV-GRY
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso SwiftClean V10 is a lightweight cordless stick vacuum that converts to handheld in seconds, for hard floors, low-pile carpet, stairs, and furniture. At 5.5 lbs with a wall-mount charging dock and LED-lit floor nozzle for illuminating debris under furniture.

## Key Features

- **180W Suction Motor**: Effective on dust, crumbs, pet hair, and lightweight debris.
- **45-Minute Runtime (Eco Mode)**: Whole-floor coverage; Max mode provides 8 minutes of boosted suction.
- **Convertible to Handheld**: Remove the tube for stairs, upholstery, and car interiors.
- **LED Floor Nozzle**: Reveals hidden debris in dark corners and under furniture.
- **0.5L Dustbin**: Tool-free release for hygienic emptying.
- **Wall-Mount Dock**: Stores and charges the vacuum; requires wall mounting near an outlet.
- **HEPA Filtration**: 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns; washable pre-filter extends HEPA life.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Battery capacity degrades naturally to approximately 70% after around 500 charge cycles. The replacement battery (HA-SCV-BAT, $59.99) is the recommended solution; the integrated battery is not field-replaceable without voiding warranty.
- The HEPA filter should be replaced every 6 months under normal use; running the vacuum with a clogged filter reduces suction and motor efficiency.
- Max mode runtime of 8 minutes limits it to spot cleaning only — not suitable for whole-floor runs.
- Not designed for high-pile carpet; suction performance degrades significantly on carpet thicker than 0.5 inches.
- Pending certifications delay availability in EU, Japan, and South Korea.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I replace the battery myself when it degrades?
A: No — the battery is integrated and opening the motor housing voids the warranty. Order the replacement battery (HA-SCV-BAT, $59.99) and have it installed by an authorized service center, or contact Contoso Support.

Q: How often should I replace the HEPA filter?
A: Every 6 months under regular use. With pets or high dust, check monthly and replace if airflow feels restricted. Rinse the washable pre-filter monthly and dry completely before reinserting.

Q: Is the SwiftClean V10 suitable for carpeted homes?
A: Low-pile carpet only (up to ~0.5 inches). Not recommended for high-pile or plush carpet where suction is significantly reduced. For carpeted homes, the CleanBot Navigator Robot Vacuum is a better primary solution.
