# CrispAir Digital Air Fryer

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso CrispAir Digital Air Fryer
Product SKU: HA-CAD-BLK (Black), HA-CAD-RED (Red)

Product Description:
The Contoso CrispAir Digital Air Fryer delivers crispy results using rapid hot-air circulation with up to 1700W of power across a 5.8-quart non-stick basket — large enough for a whole chicken or a family portion of fries. Eight preset programs and a digital touchscreen simplify operation, and the dishwasher-safe basket cuts cleanup time. Auto-shutoff and built-in overheat protection make it safe for unattended use within normal operating guidelines.

- **1700W Rapid Air Circulation**: Preheats in under 3 minutes; crispier results with significantly less oil than conventional frying.
- **8 Preset Programs**: Fries, Chicken, Fish, Steak, Vegetables, Baked Goods, Reheat, Dehydrate.
- **Digital Touchscreen**: Clear display with program icons; adjust time and temperature with a single tap.
- **Temperature Range**: 180–400°F in 5-degree increments for precise control.
- **Dishwasher-Safe Basket**: Non-stick coated basket and tray are top-rack dishwasher safe.
- **Auto-Shutoff**: Powers off when the timer ends; shake reminder alerts for programs that benefit from mid-cook tossing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much clearance does the CrispAir need during operation?
A: Maintain at least 5 inches of open space on all sides and above the unit. The exterior surface can reach 150°F, which is hot enough to damage laminate countertops, melt plastic items placed nearby, and ignite lightweight materials. Never operate it under overhead cabinets without adequate vertical clearance.

Q: Is the basket safe to put in the dishwasher?
A: Yes, the basket and tray insert are dishwasher safe on the top rack only. Avoid the bottom rack due to higher heat exposure. Do not use abrasive scrubbers or steel wool on the non-stick surface, as this will damage the coating and void the 6-month coating warranty.

Q: Can I use the CrispAir to dehydrate foods like jerky or fruit?
A: Yes. Select the Dehydrate preset, which runs at lower temperatures (typically 130–160°F) for extended periods. Dehydration cycles can take 4–8 hours depending on the food and thickness. Ensure the unit has adequate ventilation and is not left completely unattended for multi-hour cycles.
