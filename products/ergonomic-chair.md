# PostureFlex Pro Ergonomic Chair

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso PostureFlex Pro Ergonomic Office Chair
Product SKU: OP-PFP-BLK (Black), OP-PFP-GRY (Gray)
Category: Office & Productivity
Product Description:
The Contoso PostureFlex Pro is a full-featured ergonomic office chair built for professionals who spend extended hours at a desk. It combines a breathable mesh back, 4D armrests, and adjustable lumbar support to reduce fatigue and promote healthy posture. Assembly is required and takes approximately 45 minutes using included tools.

## Key Features

- **Adjustable Lumbar Support**: Height and depth adjustable lumbar pad targets the lower back precisely.
- **4D Armrests**: Move up, down, forward, backward, and pivot inward/outward for wrist and elbow alignment.
- **Breathable Mesh Back**: Open-weave mesh promotes airflow to keep users cool during long sessions.
- **Seat Depth Adjustment**: Slide the seat pan forward or back to accommodate different leg lengths.
- **135° Recline**: Smooth recline lock at multiple angles for rest or relaxed working positions.
- **Class-4 Gas Cylinder**: Heavy-duty pneumatic lift supports seat height adjustment and up to 300 lbs.
- **5-Star Aluminum Base**: Stable, scratch-resistant base with dual-wheel casters rated for hard floors and carpet.
- **High Weight Capacity**: Supports users up to 300 lbs.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Assembly is required and commonly cited as the most challenging aspect; some users report difficulty aligning the back frame bracket.
- The $50 restocking fee for assembled returns surprises some customers — review the return policy before assembly if unsure.
- Lumbar support pad is not removable; users who prefer no lumbar support have no option to detach it.
- Mesh back may feel firm initially; break-in period of 1–2 weeks is normal.
- Not available in colors beyond Black and Gray.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I return the chair after assembling it?
A: Yes, but a $50 restocking fee applies to assembled chairs. The 30-day return window still applies. If you want a full refund with no restocking fee, the chair must be disassembled and returned in its original packaging in original condition. Contoso recommends keeping packaging until you are certain you want to keep the chair.

Q: How difficult is assembly, and do I need extra tools?
A: Assembly takes approximately 45 minutes and requires only the included Allen wrench. The most common difficulty is aligning the mesh back frame to the seat base — having a second person to hold parts in place makes this significantly easier.

Q: Is the chair suitable for users over 6 feet tall?
A: The PostureFlex Pro accommodates most users between 5'4" and 6'4". Taller users may find the back height slightly short. The seat height range of 17"–21" works well for standard-height desks, though very tall users may prefer a desk with height adjustment.
