# BrewMaster Elite Smart Coffee Maker

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso BrewMaster Elite Smart Coffee Maker
Product SKU: HA-BME-BLK (Matte Black), HA-BME-SST (Stainless Steel)
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso BrewMaster Elite is a 12-cup smart coffee maker with a built-in burr grinder and five brew modes including Cold Brew, designed for home baristas who want schedule-based automation without sacrificing cup quality. Wi-Fi scheduling and brew monitoring are handled through the Contoso Home app. The included thermal carafe keeps coffee hot for up to 4 hours without a heating plate.

## Key Features

- **5 Brew Modes**: Classic, Bold, Iced, Pour-Over, and Cold Brew — each mode adjusts temperature and flow rate automatically.
- **Built-In Burr Grinder**: Grinds whole beans immediately before brewing for maximum freshness.
- **12-Cup Thermal Carafe**: Double-wall insulated carafe maintains temperature without a warming plate.
- **Wi-Fi Scheduling**: Set brew times and receive ready notifications via the Contoso Home app.
- **Auto-Clean Cycle**: One-touch descaling and rinse cycle reduces maintenance time.
- **Pod Compatible**: Accepts Contoso signature coffee pods (HA-BME-POD12) as an alternative to whole beans.
- **Water Filter**: Inline filter reduces chlorine and sediment (replacement every 2 months recommended).

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I return the machine if I already opened the pod starter pack?
A: Yes, but a $15 restocking fee will be deducted from your refund if the pod starter pack has been opened or is missing from the return. To receive a full refund, the pod starter pack must be included and sealed. All other return conditions (30-day window, original packaging) still apply.

Q: How long does Cold Brew mode take?
A: Cold Brew mode runs a slow-steep cycle of approximately 8–12 hours depending on grind coarseness and desired strength. It cannot be accelerated — the lower temperature extraction is what defines cold brew. Plan to start it the night before.

Q: Does the BrewMaster Elite work with third-party coffee pods?
A: The machine is optimized for Contoso signature pods (HA-BME-POD12). Third-party K-Cup style pods are not compatible. Standard whole beans used with the built-in grinder are always an option.
