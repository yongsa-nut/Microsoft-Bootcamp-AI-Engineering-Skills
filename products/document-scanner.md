# ScanSwift Portable Document Scanner

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso ScanSwift Portable Document Scanner
Product SKU: OP-SSP-WHT
Category: Office & Productivity
Product Description:
The Contoso ScanSwift is a compact sheet-fed scanner for office and remote workers. It handles 20 sheets via ADF at 25 ppm simplex / 50 ipm duplex, with built-in OCR for searchable PDF output. Connects via USB-C or Wi-Fi and is managed through the Contoso ScanApp (Windows/macOS).

## Key Features

- **600 DPI Optical Resolution**: Captures sharp, detailed scans suitable for archiving and OCR processing.
- **20-Sheet ADF**: Automatic document feeder handles multi-page documents without manual feeding.
- **25 ppm / 50 ipm Speed**: Scans single-sided pages at 25 per minute; duplex (both sides) at 50 images per minute.
- **Built-in OCR**: Converts scans to searchable, selectable text within PDF output automatically.
- **Multiple Output Formats**: Saves to PDF, JPEG, and PNG depending on use case.
- **USB-C and Wi-Fi Connectivity**: Connect directly via USB-C cable or wirelessly over the local network.
- **Wide Media Support**: Handles standard documents, business cards, receipts, and pages up to 8.5"x14" (legal size).
- **Contoso ScanApp**: Free desktop application for Windows and macOS with workflow automation features.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Contoso ScanApp is available for Windows and macOS only. There is currently no mobile scanning app for iOS or Android; mobile device control is not supported.
- The ADF is designed for standard paper weights (20–28 lb bond). Photos, thick cardstock, embossed cards, or folded documents may cause paper jams or feeding errors.
- Wi-Fi scanning requires the ScanApp; direct scan-to-email or scan-to-cloud without a computer is not supported.
- OCR accuracy varies with document quality; faded, handwritten, or low-contrast text may produce lower accuracy results.
- USB-C cable included is USB-C to USB-C; USB-C to USB-A adapter not included (required for computers with USB-A only ports).

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the ScanSwift from my phone or tablet?
A: No. The ScanApp is Windows 10/11 and macOS 12+ only — no iOS or Android app. Wi-Fi scanning still requires a host computer running ScanApp; standalone wireless scan-to-device is not supported.

Q: What types of documents will cause jams in the ADF?
A: The ADF handles 20–28 lb bond paper. Problem media: glossy photos, cardstock over 65 lb, folded/crumpled sheets, documents with adhesive labels, and embossed cards. Use the single-sheet manual feed slot for these.

Q: What languages does the built-in OCR support?
A: Over 20 languages including English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch. CJK (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) OCR is available as a downloadable language pack in ScanApp.
