# ConnectAll 9-in-1 USB-C Hub

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso ConnectAll 9-in-1 USB-C Hub
Product SKU: OP-CA9-GRY
Category: Office & Productivity
Product Description:
The Contoso ConnectAll 9-in-1 USB-C Hub expands a single USB-C port into nine connections, including dual HDMI outputs, USB-A and USB-C data ports, SD/microSD card slots, Ethernet, and 100W power passthrough. Its aluminum shell provides effective heat dissipation during extended use. Note that the second HDMI port uses DisplayLink technology and has platform-specific limitations.

## Key Features

- **Dual HDMI Output**: First port supports 4K@60Hz natively; second port supports 4K@30Hz via DisplayLink driver.
- **100W USB-C Power Passthrough**: Charge your laptop at full speed while using all hub ports simultaneously.
- **USB-A 3.0 x2**: High-speed data transfer up to 5 Gbps per port for peripherals and drives.
- **USB-C 3.0 Data Port**: Additional USB-C for data devices (not power delivery).
- **Gigabit Ethernet**: Stable wired networking at up to 1 Gbps for reliable connections.
- **SD and microSD Slots**: Simultaneous card reading; supports UHS-I speeds up to 104 MB/s.
- **Aluminum Shell**: Durable build with passive heat dissipation to reduce throttling.
- **Bus-Powered**: No external power required; powered through the host USB-C port.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- The second HDMI port uses DisplayLink and is NOT compatible with macOS for dual external monitor setups. macOS does not natively support DisplayLink; a third-party driver is available but has known stability issues. macOS users who need two external monitors should use a Thunderbolt dock instead.
- SD and microSD slots do not support UHS-II speeds; maximum read speed is approximately 104 MB/s regardless of card rating.
- Bus-powered design may cause instability when driving two 4K monitors and multiple high-draw USB devices simultaneously; using a powered USB hub for peripherals mitigates this.
- Not compatible with USB-C ports that are power-only (charging-only ports without data/DisplayPort Alt Mode).

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use both HDMI ports for dual external monitors on a MacBook?
A: Not reliably. The first HDMI port works on all platforms including macOS. The second HDMI port uses DisplayLink technology, which macOS does not natively support. While a third-party DisplayLink driver exists for macOS, it has documented stability issues including display flickering and system sleep/wake problems. Mac users who need dual external monitors should use a Thunderbolt 3/4 dock that supports dual native HDMI or DisplayPort outputs.

Q: Does the hub support UHS-II SD cards?
A: No. The SD and microSD slots are limited to UHS-I speeds, with a maximum read speed of approximately 104 MB/s. UHS-II cards will work in the slots but will operate at UHS-I speeds only. For UHS-II performance, a dedicated card reader is required.

Q: Will this hub work if my laptop's USB-C port is charging-only?
A: No. The ConnectAll hub requires a USB-C port with data and DisplayPort Alt Mode support. Charging-only USB-C ports do not provide the data bandwidth needed to operate the hub. Check your laptop's USB-C port specifications before purchasing.
