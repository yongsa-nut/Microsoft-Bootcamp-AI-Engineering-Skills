## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I wash the HEPA filter?
A: No. The HEPA H13 filter must be replaced. The pre-filter screen can be vacuumed gently every 2-4 weeks.

Q: Is it effective against wildfire smoke?
A: The HEPA H13 filter captures PM2.5 from wildfire smoke. Run on High with windows closed. The carbon filter helps with smoke odor but saturates faster under heavy smoke.

Q: How do I know when to replace the filter?
A: The LED indicator turns red and the app sends a notification. Check remaining filter life under Device Settings > Filter Status.
