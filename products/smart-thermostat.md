# ClimateSense Pro Smart Thermostat

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso ClimateSense Pro Smart Thermostat
Product SKU: HA-CSP-WHT
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso ClimateSense Pro is a learning smart thermostat with a 3.5-inch color touchscreen, geofencing, and detailed energy reporting. It adapts to your routine over the first two weeks of use and adjusts heating and cooling schedules automatically. Compatible with most North American HVAC systems and controllable via the Contoso Home app, Alexa, or Google Home.

## Key Features

- **Learning Schedule**: Observes your adjustments over 1–2 weeks and builds an automatic schedule without manual programming.
- **Geofencing**: Uses your phone's location to start heating or cooling before you arrive home and reduce usage when you leave.
- **3.5" Color Touchscreen**: Responsive display shows current temp, target temp, humidity, and schedule at a glance.
- **Built-In Humidity Sensor**: Monitors relative humidity and displays it alongside temperature data in the app.
- **Energy Reports**: Weekly and monthly breakdowns of heating and cooling runtime sent to the app.
- **Contoso Home App**: Remote control, schedule editing, and energy history from anywhere.
- **Alexa & Google Home**: Full voice control support. Apple HomeKit is not supported.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Does not support 5GHz Wi-Fi — 2.4GHz only. Ensure your router broadcasts a 2.4GHz network.
- Apple HomeKit is not supported. Voice control is limited to Alexa and Google Home.
- Not compatible with millivolt or gravity furnace systems. Also not compatible with 2-wire (heat-only) systems unless the included C-wire adapter is installed.
- Only available in the US and Canada — international HVAC wiring standards and system types differ significantly.
- The learning algorithm requires consistent human behavior to build an accurate schedule; irregular schedules may result in suboptimal automation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will the ClimateSense Pro work with my existing wiring?
A: It is compatible with most 24V HVAC systems, including single-stage heat/cool, multi-stage, and heat pump systems. It is NOT compatible with millivolt or gravity furnace systems. If your current thermostat has only 2 wires (R and W), you will need to install the included C-wire power adapter before wiring in the ClimateSense Pro. Check your current thermostat's wire labels before purchase.

Q: My home Wi-Fi is 5GHz — will that work?
A: No. The ClimateSense Pro only connects to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi networks. If your router broadcasts only 5GHz, or if your mesh network automatically assigns the 5GHz band, you will need to enable a 2.4GHz band or SSID on your router before setup.

Q: Does it support Apple HomeKit?
A: No. The ClimateSense Pro integrates with Alexa and Google Home only. Apple HomeKit support is not available and is not on the current product roadmap.
