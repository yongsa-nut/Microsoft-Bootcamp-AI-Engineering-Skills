## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this lamp with a smart plug or timer?
A: Yes. The lamp remembers its last brightness and color temperature setting, so when power is restored via a smart plug or timer, it resumes at those settings. However, the touch controls must be used to change settings directly; voice control integration is not supported.

Q: Is the USB-A port safe to leave plugged in overnight for charging?
A: Yes. The port includes overcurrent and overvoltage protection. It will stop delivering power once a connected device is fully charged, making overnight charging safe for standard phones and accessories.

Q: Does the auto-dimming feature work in all environments?
A: The ambient sensor works best in stable lighting environments. In rooms with rapidly changing light (e.g., near windows with passing clouds or flickering lights), the sensor may adjust more frequently than desired. Auto-dimming can be disabled by holding the brightness button for 3 seconds.
