- **Firmware Update Voids Return Eligibility**: Returns are not accepted after a firmware update has been applied. If you are considering a return, check the shaver's firmware version in the Settings menu before applying any update.

- Rotary shavers generally require a 2-3 week adaptation period for users switching from foil shavers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I return the shaver after applying the firmware update?
A: No. Once a firmware update has been applied, the unit is no longer eligible for return. The firmware update adjusts motor speed profiles and is permanently linked to the device serial number. Before updating, check the current firmware version in the Settings menu if there is any chance you may want to return the product.

Q: How often should I replace the shaving heads?
A: Contoso recommends replacing the shaving heads annually for best performance. The PC-SGR-RH3 3-pack costs $29.99 and covers one full replacement cycle. Signs of wear include reduced closeness or increased skin irritation.

Q: Is the shaver safe to use in the shower?
A: Yes. The SmoothGlide is rated IPX7, meaning it can be fully submerged in up to 1 meter of water for up to 30 minutes. It is safe for wet shaving in the shower with foam, gel, or water.
