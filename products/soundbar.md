# CinemaBar 300 Soundbar

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso CinemaBar 300 Soundbar
Product SKU: EL-CB3-BLK (Black)
Category: Electronics
Product Description:
The Contoso CinemaBar 300 is a 2.1-channel soundbar with a built-in subwoofer delivering 150W of total output across 32 inches. It connects to TVs via HDMI ARC, optical, or 3.5mm aux, and streams audio wirelessly over Bluetooth 5.0. Designed for living rooms and dedicated media rooms, it supports Dolby Digital 5.1 decode and DTS — but does not support Dolby Atmos or Wi-Fi streaming.

## Key Features

- **150W 2.1 Channel Output**: Dedicated woofer driver built in for deep bass without an external subwoofer unit.
- **HDMI ARC**: Single-cable TV connection with remote volume control via TV remote (CEC supported).
- **Bluetooth 5.0**: Stable wireless audio streaming from phones, tablets, and laptops up to 33ft.
- **Optical and Aux Inputs**: Connects to older TVs or secondary audio sources without HDMI.
- **Dolby Digital & DTS Decoding**: Processes encoded surround sound from streaming services and Blu-ray.
- **Wall-Mountable**: Includes mounting hardware for clean, bracket-free TV wall setups.
- **32-Inch Form Factor**: Proportionate with most 55–75 inch TVs without overwhelming the setup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the CinemaBar 300 support Dolby Atmos?
A: No. The CinemaBar 300 decodes Dolby Digital 5.1 and DTS but does not support Dolby Atmos. When receiving an Atmos-encoded signal via HDMI ARC or optical, the soundbar will down-mix and play it as standard Dolby Digital. For true Atmos with height channel reproduction, a separate Atmos-capable system is required.

Q: Can I stream music from Spotify or Apple Music directly over Wi-Fi?
A: No. The CinemaBar 300 does not have Wi-Fi capability and does not support Spotify Connect, AirPlay, or similar network audio protocols. To stream from music apps, connect your phone or tablet via Bluetooth 5.0 and play audio through the Bluetooth connection.

Q: Do I need a separate subwoofer?
A: No. The CinemaBar 300 has a built-in subwoofer driver within the soundbar chassis, handling bass frequencies without requiring an external sub. It is not compatible with an add-on external subwoofer.
