## Known Issues & Limitations

- Does not charge smartwatches — Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy Watch, and other smartwatch wireless standards are not supported. Phone Qi charging only.
- iPhones charge at a maximum of 7.5W, not MagSafe 15W. Apple MagSafe speed requires a MagSafe-certified charger.

- Charging two devices simultaneously may result in slightly longer total charge times versus two individual chargers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I charge my Apple Watch or Samsung Galaxy Watch on this pad?
A: No. The ChargePad Duo uses the Qi wireless standard, which is designed for smartphones and Qi-compatible earbuds. Apple Watch requires Apple's proprietary magnetic charging protocol, and Samsung Galaxy Watch requires its own charging standard. Neither is compatible with Qi pads.

Q: Will it charge my iPhone at 15W MagSafe speed?
A: No. The ChargePad Duo is a Qi-certified charger, not a MagSafe charger. iPhones will charge at up to 7.5W — faster than standard 5W Qi, but not at the 15W MagSafe maximum. For 15W charging, an Apple-certified MagSafe charger is required.

Q: My phone case is silicone — will it still work?
A: Most standard silicone and thin plastic cases up to 5mm thick are compatible. Charging may fail or be inconsistent with cases thicker than 5mm, or any case containing metal components or plates, which interfere with the wireless field.
