## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the ViewFlex 15.6 work with any USB-C port?
A: No. The USB-C connection requires DisplayPort Alt Mode support on the host device's port. Many USB-C ports — especially on older laptops or ports labeled as charging-only — do not support DisplayPort Alt Mode. Check your device's specifications for "USB-C with DisplayPort" or "Thunderbolt" before purchasing. If your USB-C ports lack this support, use the included mini-HDMI cable with a separate USB-C power source.

Q: Does the monitor include its own power supply?
A: No. The ViewFlex draws power directly from the host laptop or device through the USB-C cable. Most modern laptops supply sufficient USB-C power. If you are connecting via mini-HDMI, you will need to supply power separately through a USB-C power adapter (not included) connected to the monitor's USB-C port.

Q: Why is the return window only 14 days instead of 30?
A: Display panels require a physical inspection process to verify condition upon return, which Contoso performs within a 14-day evaluation window. If you receive the monitor and want to verify compatibility before committing, Contoso recommends testing all connections within the first 48 hours and contacting support if issues arise.
