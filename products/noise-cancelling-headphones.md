## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why don't the HushElite headphones support aptX or LDAC?
A: SBC and AAC only. AAC provides near-CD quality for Apple devices; SBC is the universal fallback. For most AAC-capable devices (iPhone, iPad, newer Android flagships), the difference is negligible. Consider this before purchase if aptX/LDAC is a hard requirement.

Q: How does multipoint Bluetooth work in practice?
A: After pairing two devices, audio from the active device takes priority. Calls on the second device trigger automatic switching. Only one audio stream plays at a time. Call switching is automatic; media switching may require manual pause/play.

Q: What is the restocking fee policy for returns?
A: 15% restocking fee on any opened return (factory seal broken), regardless of usage. Returns for confirmed manufacturing defects are exempt. Inspect packaging before opening and contact support first if you suspect a defect.
