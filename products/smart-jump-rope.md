# SkipSync Digital Jump Rope

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso SkipSync Digital Jump Rope
Product SKU: FW-SSD-BLK
Category: Fitness & Wearables
Product Description:
The Contoso SkipSync Digital Jump Rope tracks jumps, calories burned, and session time via an LCD display built into the handle. Bluetooth 5.0 syncs your workout data to the Contoso FitLife app for progress tracking over time. A set of weighted cordless balls is included for indoor practice without an actual rope.

## Key Features

- **LCD Handle Display**: View jumps, calories, and elapsed time at a glance without checking your phone.
- **Bluetooth 5.0 Sync**: Connects to the Contoso FitLife app for session history and trend data.
- **Adjustable Cable**: Steel-core cable adjusts up to 9.8 ft to accommodate different heights.
- **Smooth Ball Bearings**: Precision bearings in the handles ensure tangle-free, consistent rotation.
- **USB-C Rechargeable**: Up to 20 hours of battery life per charge.
- **Cordless Indoor Mode**: Swap in the included weighted balls to practice jump timing indoors without swinging a rope.
- **Calorie Estimation**: Calorie burn calculated based on jump count and user weight set in the FitLife app.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Strava sync is not supported. Workout data stays within the Contoso FitLife ecosystem only.
- Apple Watch integration is not available. The rope connects directly to the FitLife app on your phone via Bluetooth — no watch app or widget exists.
- Calorie estimates are approximations based on jump count and are not clinically precise.
- The cable may scuff hardwood or polished tile floors during use — use on a mat or outdoors when possible.
- Bluetooth connection can occasionally drop if the phone is more than 20 ft away during outdoor sessions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the SkipSync sync with Strava, Garmin Connect, or Apple Watch?
A: No. The SkipSync Digital Jump Rope only syncs with the Contoso FitLife app via Bluetooth 5.0. There is no native integration with Strava, Garmin Connect, Apple Watch, or other third-party fitness platforms. If you need workout data in those platforms, you would need to log it manually.

Q: How does the cordless mode work, and is it as effective as using the rope?
A: The cordless mode replaces the cable with two weighted balls that you rotate with your wrists, simulating the motion and timing of jumping rope. The handles still track your "jump" count via the motion sensors. It is effective for practicing rhythm and coordination and for use in small indoor spaces. Calorie burn in cordless mode is lower than with an actual rope since there is no cable to turn, and the handle sensors estimate jumps from wrist motion only.

Q: Can I use the rope without the app?
A: Yes. The LCD display on the handles shows jumps, calories, and time independently — no phone or app connection is required during a session. The app is only needed to view historical data, set user weight for calorie calculations, and access any FitLife workout programs. The rope functions as a standalone device without Bluetooth active.
