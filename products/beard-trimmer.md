## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this trimmer in the shower?
A: Yes. The EdgeMaster is rated IPX7, meaning it is safe for use under running water and in the shower. After use, rinse the blade head under the tap with the guide comb removed for easy cleaning. Fully dry the unit before storing.

Q: Does the trimmer work on body hair, not just facial hair?
A: Yes. The guide combs and the main blade are suitable for body grooming. The detail trimmer head is best suited for fine facial precision work. For body use, start with a longer guard setting and work shorter if needed.

Q: What is the difference between the three included guide combs?
A: The three combs are sized for short (approximately 3-6mm), medium (approximately 7-12mm), and long (approximately 13-20mm) beard lengths. The comb snaps onto the blade head and limits cut depth. For lengths below 3mm, remove the comb and use the length adjustment dial directly on the blade head.
