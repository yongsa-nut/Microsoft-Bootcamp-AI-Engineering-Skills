# BodySync Smart Scale

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso BodySync Smart Scale
Product SKU: FW-BSS-WHT (White), FW-BSS-BLK (Black)

Product Description:
The Contoso BodySync Smart Scale delivers comprehensive body composition tracking beyond basic weight measurement. It connects to the Contoso FitLife app via Bluetooth to log and visualize your fitness data over time. With support for up to 8 user profiles, it's ideal for households with multiple fitness-minded members.

## Key Features

- **Body Composition Analysis**: Measures weight, BMI, body fat %, muscle mass, bone mass, body water %, and visceral fat using bioelectrical impedance.
- **Multi-User Support**: Stores up to 8 individual user profiles with automatic recognition.
- **App Integration**: Syncs wirelessly to the Contoso FitLife app; iOS syncs to Apple Health, Android syncs to Google Fit.
- **Tempered Glass Platform**: Durable, easy-to-clean surface built for daily use.
- **High Weight Capacity**: Supports users up to 400 lbs (181 kg).
- **Instant Auto-On**: Steps on and the scale activates automatically — no buttons required.
- **Trend Tracking**: View historical data and progress graphs directly in the FitLife app.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Body composition readings (body fat %, muscle mass, bone mass) are estimates via bioelectrical impedance and are NOT medical-grade — not a substitute for DEXA scan or professional assessment.
- Do NOT use this scale if you have a pacemaker or any implanted electronic medical device — the bioelectrical impedance signal may interfere.
- Apple Health sync is iOS only; Android users sync to Google Fit. Cross-platform sync is not supported.
- Manual weight entry is not available in the FitLife app — the scale must be used to log data.
- Readings can vary based on hydration level, time of day, and foot placement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are the body fat and muscle mass readings accurate enough to track my fitness progress?
A: The readings use bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA), which provides useful trend data over time but should not be treated as clinically precise measurements. Day-to-day fluctuations are normal due to hydration and timing. For tracking general fitness progress week-over-week, most users find it sufficient. For medical or clinical assessments, consult a physician and consider a DEXA scan.

Q: Can I use this scale if I have a pacemaker?
A: No. The BodySync Smart Scale uses a small bioelectrical current to measure body composition. This signal can potentially interfere with pacemakers and other implanted electronic medical devices. Do not use the body composition features — or the scale at all — if you have such a device. Consult your physician for safe alternatives.

Q: Does the Android app sync with Apple Health?
A: No. Apple Health is an iOS-exclusive platform and is not accessible from Android devices. On iOS, the FitLife app can sync data to Apple Health. On Android, the app syncs to Google Fit instead. There is no cross-platform health app integration.
