## Known Issues & Limitations

- The NailPro is NOT suitable for acrylic nail removal or gel nail removal. Removing acrylics or gel requires professional-grade drill bits and torque levels that this consumer kit does not provide. Attempting to use it for this purpose may damage the attachments or the motor.
- Attachment bits are made from standard abrasive materials and will wear over time with frequent use. Replacement attachment sets are not currently available separately — contact support for options if bits become worn.
- The LED work light is helpful but not a substitute for adequate overhead lighting in detailed cuticle work.
- Not yet approved for several markets — see Regional Availability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this kit to remove acrylic or gel nails?
A: No. The NailPro is designed for natural nail care — filing, buffing, shaping, and cuticle maintenance. Acrylic and gel removal requires professional-grade drill bits, significantly higher torque, and controlled technique to avoid nail bed damage. Using the NailPro for this purpose is not recommended and may damage the unit. Visit a nail technician for acrylic or gel removal.

Q: Is this kit suitable for toenails as well as fingernails?
A: Yes. The filing and shaping attachments work on both fingernails and toenails. For thicker toenails, start at a medium speed setting and use the coarser filing attachment. The cuticle attachments are sized for fingernail work but can be used carefully on toenails as well.

Q: How do I clean the attachments between uses?
A: Wipe attachments with a dry or slightly damp cloth after each use to remove nail dust. For deeper cleaning, use a small brush (like the cleaning brush from the Contoso EdgeMaster Trimmer kit) to clear debris from the abrasive surface. Do not soak attachments in liquid or place them in a dishwasher.
