# GlidePro Ergonomic Wireless Mouse

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso GlidePro Ergonomic Wireless Mouse
Product SKU: EL-GPE-BLK (Black), EL-GPE-GRY (Gray), EL-GPE-WHT (Pearl White — Discontinued)
Category: Electronics
Product Description:
The Contoso GlidePro is an ergonomically shaped wireless mouse designed to reduce wrist strain during extended computing sessions. It supports both 2.4GHz USB dongle and Bluetooth 5.1 connections, offers adjustable DPI up to 4000, and delivers up to 90 days of use per charge via USB-C. Pearl White (EL-GPE-WHT) has been discontinued as of February 2025 and is no longer covered by Contoso warranty.

## Key Features

- **Ergonomic Vertical-Tilt Design**: Angled grip reduces forearm pronation and wrist fatigue during long sessions.
- **Dual Connectivity**: Switch between 2.4GHz USB dongle and Bluetooth 5.1 for use across multiple devices.
- **Adjustable 4000 DPI**: Four DPI presets (800/1600/2400/4000) cycle via dedicated button for precision control.
- **6 Programmable Buttons**: Includes forward/back thumb buttons configurable via Contoso Mouse software.
- **Silent Click Mechanism**: Reduced-noise switches offer quieter operation in shared office environments.
- **USB-C Rechargeable**: 90-day battery life; 2-hour full charge via USB-C, works wired while charging.
- **Cross-Platform Compatible**: Windows 10/11, macOS 12 and later (including macOS Sonoma), ChromeOS, Linux.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Pearl White (EL-GPE-WHT) is discontinued as of February 2025. Any remaining Pearl White units sold through third-party retailers are not eligible for Contoso warranty service.
- The USB dongle occupies a USB-A port; users with USB-C-only devices will need a USB-A adapter (not included).
- DPI button cycles only forward through presets — there is no reverse-cycle option without the software utility.
- Bluetooth connection may require re-pairing after extended idle periods on some older macOS versions prior to Sonoma.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the GlidePro suitable for left-handed users?
A: No. The GlidePro features an asymmetric ergonomic shape specifically contoured for right-hand grip. The thumb buttons are positioned on the left side of the mouse, making it uncomfortable and impractical for left-handed use. Contoso does not currently offer a left-handed variant.

Q: Does it work with macOS Sonoma?
A: Yes. macOS Sonoma compatibility was confirmed and documented in firmware version 1.2. Both Bluetooth 5.1 and 2.4GHz dongle modes function correctly. The Contoso Mouse software for button customization is available for macOS 12 and later, including Sonoma.

Q: What is the warranty status for Pearl White units?
A: Contoso discontinued the Pearl White (EL-GPE-WHT) variant in February 2025. Units purchased directly from Contoso before that date remain under the standard 24-month warranty. Pearl White units purchased from third-party sellers after February 2025 are outside Contoso's warranty coverage regardless of purchase date.
