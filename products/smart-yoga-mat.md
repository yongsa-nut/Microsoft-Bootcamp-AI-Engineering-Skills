# ZenFlow Interactive Yoga Mat

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso ZenFlow Interactive Yoga Mat
Product SKU: FW-ZFM-GRY (Gray), FW-ZFM-PUR (Purple)
Category: Fitness & Wearables
Product Description:
The Contoso ZenFlow Interactive Yoga Mat brings real-time pose feedback to your practice through embedded pressure sensors connected to the ZenFlow app. Guided yoga and Pilates programs adapt to your alignment and balance performance. A USB-C rechargeable battery powers up to 10 sessions per charge, and the mat works as a standard mat when unpowered.

## Key Features

- **Pose Alignment Feedback**: Embedded pressure sensors detect body position and flag alignment issues in real time via the ZenFlow app.
- **Guided Programs**: Access structured yoga and Pilates sessions with progressive difficulty levels.
- **Wi-Fi Connectivity**: Connects to your home Wi-Fi network — router proximity required during use.
- **USB-C Rechargeable**: Built-in battery lasts approximately 10 sessions per charge.
- **Non-Slip TPE Surface**: 6mm thick mat with textured, grippy surface for safe practice on hard floors.
- **Passive Mode**: Functions as a regular yoga mat with no smart features when battery is depleted.
- **Dual Color Options**: Available in Gray (FW-ZFM-GRY) and Purple (FW-ZFM-PUR).

## Known Issues & Limitations

- The mat uses Wi-Fi only — there is no Bluetooth mode. You must be within range of your home router during use; smart features will not function without an active Wi-Fi connection.
- Not compatible with third-party yoga apps (e.g., Glo, Alo Moves). The ZenFlow app is required.
- When the battery is dead, all smart features are unavailable. The mat still functions as a regular yoga mat.
- The mat cannot be folded for storage — rolling only. Folding may damage embedded sensor wiring.
- Lower review count (456) means long-term durability data is still limited.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use this mat with Glo, Alo Moves, or other yoga apps?
A: No. The ZenFlow Interactive Yoga Mat only integrates with the Contoso ZenFlow app. The sensor data and real-time feedback pipeline is proprietary to that app. If you use a third-party yoga app for guided sessions, the mat will still function physically as a non-smart mat, but no pose feedback or tracking will occur.

Q: Does the mat need Wi-Fi to work, or can I use it offline?
A: Wi-Fi is required for all smart features. The mat connects to your 2.4GHz home network and streams data to the ZenFlow app in real time. If you take the mat somewhere without Wi-Fi (a park, hotel, etc.), or if your router is too far away, smart features will be unavailable. The mat itself still works as a regular yoga mat in those situations.

Q: What happens when the battery dies mid-session?
A: The mat will lose all smart functionality — pose tracking, alignment feedback, and app connectivity will stop. The physical mat remains usable for the rest of your session. Recharge via USB-C before your next smart session. Battery level is shown in the ZenFlow app before you begin.
