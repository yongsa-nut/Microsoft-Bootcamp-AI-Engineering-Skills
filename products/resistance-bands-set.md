## Known Issues & Limitations

- Bands will degrade over time with heavy use — inspect regularly for nicks, tears, or discoloration before each session.
- Not recommended for use in extreme cold (below 32°F / 0°C) as TPE may become brittle.
- Door anchor is designed for standard interior doors; not recommended for use with hollow-core doors under heavy loads.
- The 50 lb band requires significant anchor stability — ensure door anchor placement is secure before applying full tension.
- Stackable resistance is approximate; exact combined resistance varies with stretch distance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are these bands safe for people with latex allergies?
A: Yes. The FlexForce bands are made entirely from TPE (Thermoplastic Elastomer), which contains no natural latex. They are a purpose-built alternative for users with latex sensitivities or allergies. However, if you have severe allergies, check with your physician before use, as manufacturing facilities may vary.

Q: Can I combine multiple bands on a single handle for more resistance?
A: Yes. All five bands are compatible with the included handles and ankle straps simultaneously. You can stack any combination for resistance between 10 and 150 lbs. The carabiner-style clips on each handle accept multiple bands. Ensure your anchor point (door anchor or fixed object) is stable before using high combined resistance.

Q: How do I know when to replace a band?
A: Inspect bands before each session. Replace a band if you notice visible cracks, white stress marks, nicks along the edge, or any thinning in the material. Do not use a damaged band — breakage under tension can cause injury. Replacement bands are available separately as FW-FFB-RPL for $14.99.
