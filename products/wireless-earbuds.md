## Known Issues & Limitations

- ANC may produce a faint hissing or pressure sensation in very quiet environments — this is normal behavior for hybrid ANC systems
- No wireless charging support on the case; USB-C only
- Connects to one device at a time — multipoint Bluetooth is not supported
- Ear tip fit significantly affects bass response and ANC effectiveness; try all included sizes
- Firmware updates require the Contoso Audio companion app (iOS/Android); app is not available on desktop

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use one earbud at a time?
A: Yes, either earbud functions independently for mono listening or calls. The other earbud remains in the case and charges. Battery life in single-earbud mode is approximately 8.5 hours since ANC processing load is reduced.

Q: Does the case support wireless (Qi) charging?
A: No. The SonicBuds Pro case charges exclusively via USB-C. If wireless charging is a requirement, consider the Contoso HushElite headphones, which offer a broader accessory ecosystem.

Q: Can I connect to my laptop and phone at the same time?
A: No. The SonicBuds Pro supports single-device Bluetooth only. To switch devices, you must disconnect from the current device first — either through your device's Bluetooth settings or by holding the touch control for 3 seconds to enter pairing mode.
