## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the RecoverPro on a pulled muscle or fresh injury?
A: No. You should not use a percussion massager on acute injuries, including muscle strains, sprains, tears, bruises, or any area that is swollen or inflamed. Percussive force on damaged tissue can worsen the injury. Allow the acute phase to pass (typically 48–72 hours with no active inflammation) and consult a physician or physiotherapist before resuming massage therapy. This device is intended for general muscle recovery, not injury rehabilitation.

Q: How often do the attachment heads need to be replaced?
A: Attachment head lifespan depends on frequency and intensity of use. With daily use at high speeds, heads may show wear within 3–6 months. Inspect heads before each use — replace if the foam or rubber surface is cracking, compressed flat, or detaching. Replacement sets (FW-RPM-AH6) include all 6 head types for $19.99. Using worn heads reduces effectiveness and may be uncomfortable on skin.

Q: Is the noise level actually low enough for apartment or office use?
A: At speed level 1 (1,200 RPM), the RecoverPro operates at approximately 45dB — roughly the volume of a quiet conversation. Most users find this acceptable in shared living spaces. At speed 5 (3,200 RPM), noise increases to approximately 65dB, which is more noticeable in quiet environments. For considerate use around others, keep the device at speeds 1–2 in shared spaces.
