The Contoso StreamCam 4K Webcam delivers sharp, professional-quality video for streaming, video conferencing, and content creation. ## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why can't I return the webcam after updating the firmware?
A: The firmware update applies a calibration process that personalizes the image processing pipeline — including autofocus tuning and exposure curves — to the specific unit's sensor characteristics. This process is irreversible and unique per unit, which means the camera cannot be repackaged or resold in a standardized state. Contoso strongly recommends fully evaluating the webcam before applying any firmware updates.

Q: Does the StreamCam 4K work on Linux?
A: No. The StreamCam 4K is compatible with Windows 10 and later and macOS 12 (Monterey) and later only. Linux is not supported and there is no planned Linux driver release at this time. Users on Linux should consider a UVC-class webcam with generic driver support instead.

Q: Does 4K mode work on any USB port?
A: No. 4K at 30fps requires a USB 3.0 or higher port to sustain the necessary data bandwidth. Plugging into a USB 2.0 port causes the camera to automatically fall back to 1080p resolution. Check your computer's port specifications if you are not seeing 4K output.
