# BrewMaster Elite Smart Coffee Maker

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso BrewMaster Elite Smart Coffee Maker
Product SKU: HA-BME-BLK (Matte Black), HA-BME-SST (Stainless Steel)
Category: Home Appliances
Product Description:
The Contoso BrewMaster Elite is a 10-cup smart coffee maker with a built-in burr grinder and four brew modes for a freshly ground, customized cup every time. Wi-Fi scheduling and brew monitoring are handled through the Contoso Home app. The included thermal carafe keeps coffee hot for up to 4 hours without a heating plate.

## Key Features

- **Built-In Burr Grinder**: Grinds whole beans fresh before each brew for maximum flavor
- **4 Brew Modes**: Classic, Bold, Iced, and Pour-Over to suit different preferences
- **10-Cup Thermal Carafe**: Double-wall insulated carafe maintains temperature without a warming plate
- **Wi-Fi & App Control**: Schedule brews, adjust strength, and monitor status via Contoso Home app
- **Auto-Clean Cycle**: One-touch descaling and rinse cycle reduces maintenance time

## Known Issues & Limitations

- No cold brew mode; Iced mode brews hot and pours over ice, which differs from true cold brew
- App requires iOS 14+ or Android 10+; older devices may not be compatible
- The grinder is not user-serviceable; grinding issues require contacting Contoso support
- Water hardness in some regions may require more frequent descaling than the auto-clean reminder suggests
- Pod and whole-bean modes cannot be used simultaneously — select one mode per brew cycle

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this coffee maker support cold brew?
A: No. The BrewMaster Elite does not have a cold brew mode. The Iced brew mode brews a concentrated hot coffee that is designed to be poured over ice.

Q: Can I use pre-ground coffee instead of whole beans?
A: Yes. You can bypass the grinder by adding pre-ground coffee directly to the filter basket. The machine will brew normally without activating the grinder.

Q: Is the water reservoir dishwasher safe?
A: The removable water reservoir is top-rack dishwasher safe. The carafe and filter basket are also dishwasher safe. Do not place the main unit in water.
