## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this straightener safe to use in other countries?
A: Yes, for most countries. The SilkPress Pro supports dual voltage (110-240V), so it works with standard outlets across the US, Europe, Australia, and most other regions. However, Japan uses a different plug type that is not compatible, and an adapter is not included. Check local plug standards before traveling.

Q: What does the auto-shutoff do, and can it be disabled?
A: The auto-shutoff powers the unit off after 60 minutes of inactivity. This is a fixed safety feature and cannot be disabled. If your styling session exceeds 60 minutes, simply switch the unit back on. The heat-up time is only 15 seconds, so resuming is quick.

Q: Can I use this on color-treated or chemically processed hair?
A: Yes, with care. Use lower temperature settings (250-320°F) for color-treated, bleached, or chemically relaxed hair to minimize further damage. Higher settings are better suited to thick, coarse, or natural hair. Always use a heat protectant product before styling.
