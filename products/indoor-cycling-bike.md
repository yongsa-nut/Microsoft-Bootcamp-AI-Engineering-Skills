# SpinConnect Pro Indoor Cycling Bike

## Product Overview

Product Name: Contoso SpinConnect Pro Indoor Cycling Bike
Product SKU: FW-SCP-BLK
Category: Fitness & Wearables
Product Description:
The Contoso SpinConnect Pro is a connected indoor cycling bike with a 22 lb flywheel and 100-level magnetic resistance. A 21.5-inch HD touchscreen delivers live and on-demand classes via the SpinConnect subscription. Bluetooth and ANT+ connect heart rate monitors, cadence sensors, and third-party apps.

## Key Features

- **22 lb Flywheel**: Road-like momentum and smooth deceleration.
- **100-Level Magnetic Resistance**: Silent adjustment via touchscreen or manual knob.
- **21.5" HD Touchscreen**: SpinConnect classes, metrics, and performance tracking.
- **Bluetooth + ANT+**: Heart rate monitors, cadence sensors, and third-party app connectivity.
- **SPD-Compatible Pedals**: Accepts SPD cleats; toe cage straps included for regular shoes.
- **Adjustable Fit**: Seat and handlebars adjust horizontally and vertically for a wide range of rider heights.
- **Bluetooth Audio Out**: Wireless headphones or Bluetooth speaker — no built-in speakers.

## Known Issues & Limitations

- Not compatible with the Peloton app — Peloton's platform cannot be sideloaded onto the touchscreen.
- Zwift has no native support. ANT+ can connect to Zwift via a dongle on a separate device (e.g., laptop), but this is unofficial and requires user setup.
- No built-in speakers — Bluetooth audio out only.
- Without a SpinConnect subscription, manual mode only: resistance control and basic metrics (time, cadence, estimated watts). No class library, leaderboard, or workout history.
- At 135 lbs, two people needed for placement. Professional assembly recommended.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What can I do on the bike without a SpinConnect subscription?
A: Manual mode only: set resistance across all 100 levels, track time, cadence, and estimated power, and connect heart rate monitors via Bluetooth/ANT+. No access to class library, instructor-led rides, leaderboard, or workout history. Subscription is $9.99/month and can be cancelled anytime.

Q: Is this bike compatible with Peloton or Zwift?
A: Not compatible with Peloton — their platform is exclusive to Peloton hardware. For Zwift, the bike's ANT+ signal can be picked up by a separate device (laptop with ANT+ USB dongle) running Zwift. This workaround is not officially supported and requires user configuration.

Q: Does the bike include everything I need to start riding?
A: Pedals with SPD cleat compatibility and toe cage straps are included. You need your own wireless headphones or Bluetooth speaker (no built-in audio). A floor mat is included. Assembly requires two people and 45–60 minutes.
